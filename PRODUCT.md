# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

City transport leaders, investors, institutional partners, operators, and passengers evaluating Kencid Mobility's universal design transport systems. Visitors are looking for confidence that the company can deliver inclusive, accessible, reliable mobility infrastructure.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Kencid Mobility as an African mobility company designing and manufacturing universal design vehicles, terminals, and digital passenger systems. Success means visitors quickly understand the offer, see the accessibility proof points, and feel enough trust to contact or partner with the company.

## Brand Personality

Purposeful, engineered, inclusive. The voice should feel confident and practical, with visible care for dignity, safety, and access.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic transport landing pages, decorative technology cliches, inaccessible low-contrast visuals, and layouts that discuss inclusion without showing real accessible features.

## Design Principles

Show the physical system, not abstract promises.
Keep accessibility visible as core infrastructure.
Use strong contrast, direct copy, and focused sections.
Preserve the existing lime, navy, white, and image-led Kencid identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessibility is a core brand promise. Maintain readable contrast, meaningful alt text, keyboard-friendly controls, reduced-motion handling, and layouts that do not hide essential information on mobile.
