Who We Are

Redefining Movement, By Design.

KENCID Mobility Ltd is a bold African mobility company redefining transportation through Universal Design, manufacturing, innovation and technology. We design, engineer and manufacture vehicles, infrastructure and smart mobility systems so every passenger can move with dignity, independence and equal participation; because inclusion should never be an afterthought.
